## Evidence and boundaries

The network-partner positioning is based on the owner's stated strategy, not an
independent audit of live launch contracts. Verify it against the release catalog
and partner evidence before store submission. Multiple suppliers do not by
themselves prove that each individual eSIM can switch between mobile networks.

There is no claim of being cheapest, cheaper than a named competitor, guaranteed
savings, automatic best-network switching, universal multi-network access, a
specific network count or a speed/reliability guarantee. Value is expressed as
choice and fit for the customer's trip and budget, not a price-comparison badge.

If a comparative price claim is added later, first establish current, like-for-like
destination, allowance, validity, tax/currency, fair-use and service comparisons.
Do not infer a general price advantage from selected examples.

Google's listing guidance warns against pricing/promotional claims and calls for
screenshots to focus on the actual app experience:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723?hl=en&authuser=993749347
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en

This is creative preparation, not policy approval. All images still contain design
reference UI and sample data and require native capture replacement.
